Welcome to my personal website. I am a final-year undergraduate student at UC Berkeley.

I was born in Hainan, an island in the southernmost part of China, where I spent many of my childhood summers drinking coconut water. At 14, I moved to the U.S. and spent my teenage years in Irvine, California.

Before Berkeley, I was a self-taught artist and had never received formal art training. I had always enjoyed doodling as a hobby, but it was at Berkeley that I found my deeper passion for art and decided to add Art Practice as my major.

The main themes in my work include childish dreams, the tension between lasting moments and fading time, and the interplay between the digital and physical worlds. Through art, I chase the dreams I was not able to follow as a child, wandering through this world with a heart that still dreams.

Exhibition

Sitting in Memories Berkeley, CA
Senior Group Exhibition - Water Marks April 30 - May 19, 2026
2D animation projections and installation 8’ x 8’ x 8’
Art 185: Senior Project, 2026

Sitting in Memories explores memory as an immersive space rather than a fixed record. Inspired by large-scale digital installations, it uses abstract motion, color, light, and rhythm to evoke the feeling of remembering, inviting viewers to project their own experiences.
